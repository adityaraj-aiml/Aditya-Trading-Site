FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do I need trading experience before joining?
No prior experience is needed for Starter. Pro and Elite assume you've completed the Foundations material or can already read a basic chart.
Which platform do the indicators work on?
TradingView, written in Pine Script. A step-by-step installation guide is included, and they work on both free and paid TradingView plans.
Are the classes live or recorded?
Core curriculum is recorded so you can go at your own pace, plus scheduled live chart-review sessions each week for Pro and Elite.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes — a 7-day refund window applies if you've completed less than 20% of the course content. Full terms are shared at checkout.
Do you give buy/sell signals?
No. Techin By Raj teaches the system and gives you the tools — every decision on your own chart stays yours.
How do I get support if I'm stuck?
The Telegram community is open for daily questions, and the Elite plan includes 1:1 doubt-clearing calls.
